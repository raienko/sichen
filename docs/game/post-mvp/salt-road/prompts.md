## salt-cart-chase

Use case: ui-mockup / stylized-concept.
Create ONE portrait mobile gameplay concept ~1024x1792, full bleed, for a playful Ukrainian folk fantasy HORSE-CART SALT CHASE level. Supplied image is the visual reference only: use the same faceted low-poly 3D, matte simple surfaces, moss/olive/slate woodland, soft ambient lighting, sparse warm torch accents, thin cream circles and pictograms. No photoreal or detailed painted rendering.
Elevated third-person chase camera above/behind the PLAYER'S moving wooden four-wheel cart. The cart is pulled by ONE HORSE correctly harnessed ahead, running toward the TOP of the image. Player Cossack driver wears white shirt, a few red geometric embroidery marks, red sash/trousers, black moustache/oseledets. Clearly visible tied BURLAP SACKS OF WHITE SALT are the cargo, simple crystal stamp identifies salt. Horse pulls cart, driver sits on front bench, sacks occupy the rear bed. No modern vehicle, engine or ox team.
A winding dirt woodland trail stretches ahead between faceted trees, angular rocks, wooden fences and a small rushnyk-draped roadside shrine. Upcoming obstacles are readable: a fallen branch/log covering ONE side, a few crates on the opposite side farther up, a narrow clear passing gap. Dust trail and wheel tracks suggest speed. Cart is beginning a controlled sideways DRIFT around a bend, visibly angled relative to path, rear-wheel skid marks but still on wheels. Horse is turning with the harness, not magically sideways.
TWO OTHER HUMAN PvP PLAYERS pursue on SEPARATE RIDING HORSES, not carts. One close at the rear-right quarter, another farther behind on left. Give them simple distinct colored sashes/name glyphs and mounted silhouettes; no fantasy monsters in this chase.
The chase mechanic must be VISUALLY INFORMATIVE: the close pursuer has a small crossed-swords circular LOCK-ON PROGRESS ring about 2/3 filled, and compact readable status "6 m · 2/3 s". This illustrates holding attack distance continuously before triggering separate PvP battle. The farther rider has a faint empty indicator and small "12 m", outside range. Do not show that battle already started, health bars draining or swords hitting a cart from far away. A subtle short dotted/colored proximity arc near the close pursuer is okay, no huge glowing field covering road.
Minimal overlay HUD: upper-left small Cossack portrait, thin cart durability bar with wheel icon, below it bag/crystal icon with "7/10"; upper-right compact route bend/minimap and distant finish flag, no card panels or huge text. Lower-left understated steering/turn control. Lower-right thumb-reachable arc with two clear ivory circles: skidding-wheel/curved-track DRIFT glyph and SALT BAG THROW glyph with small "7"; optional small rear-view eye above them. Keep center clear of clickable HUD. Forward motion is automatic; steering can persist briefly while tapping a right-side action, so no mandatory simultaneous holds. Show action icons, not explanatory paragraphs.
At least one salt sack is loose at rear ready to throw. Fun tense readable chase, sense of weight/speed without excessive blur. Ukrainian folk atmosphere in costume/cloth/shrine. No battle five-action planner, Ready, tactics, six loadout slots or active-skill list. This is a speculative new mode illustration, not a claim it exists.

## salt-cart-drift-escape

Use case: ui-mockup / stylized-concept.
Create ONE LANDSCAPE 16:9 gameplay action illustration around1536x864 of the SAME playful Ukrainian folk HORSE-CART SALT CHASE concept. Use supplied reference's faceted LOW-POLY 3D, matte clean materials, muted forest greens, ochre foliage, slate rocks, soft diffuse lighting, simple model silhouettes. No photoreal textures, no realistic cinematic painting.
Elevated three-quarter rear/side chase camera at a tight dirt-road bend. Show the Cossack player's FOUR-WHEEL WOODEN CART loaded with tied BURLAP SALT SACKS, pulled by ONE correctly harnessed horse ahead. White embroidered linen, red trousers/sash, black moustache and oseledets. Cart rear is sliding sideways into a DRIFT, two clear curving skid tracks and tan dust plume, horse leaning into turn, all four wheels grounded enough for a playful racing game. Keep the route ahead visible with a gap around a fallen log and rocks; wooden fence, rushnyk shrine and faceted conifers establish the same world.
Freeze a legible BAG THROW: driver twists from the front bench and tosses ONE salt sack rearward toward a pursuing HUMAN PLAYER RIDING HIS OWN HORSE. Show the sack's short dotted arc from cart to rear rider, loose white salt/crystal puff marking a glancing impact near rider. Rider shields eyes and slows slightly, horse upright and safe; no blood, no ragdoll or falling horse. This is cargo spent to break pursuit and escape, not magical projectiles. Keep several sacks still secured in cart. A second mounted rival farther behind approaches but remains outside engagement distance.
Visually communicate the PvP proximity timer breaking: beside the hit rider, crossed-swords lock ring is partly filled amber but has a small break mark/grey gap and concise "Out of range"; a small distance "10 m" shows gap now opening. Keep information compact, no giant tutorial text or splash announcements. Player will only enter separate tactical PvP if a rival stays close long enough; no combat scene transition shown yet.
Sparse immersive overlay: upper-left portrait, wheel/durability strip and salt bag "6/10" (one cargo sack spent). Upper-right compact route bend and finish flag. Lower-left steering pad near outer corner; lower-right small curved cluster with skidding-wheel DRIFT button lit amber and bag-throw button with "6". Use thin ivory circles, quiet translucent fills, restrained amber focus matching reference; no modern speedometer or car dashboard, no separate UI cards, no central clickable element. Two outer thumb groups suit landscape/foldable play.
Composition should make horse+cart, rear slide, airborne sack/salt puff, and mounted pursuer all instantly readable. Fun low-budget feasible 3D game, Ukrainian salt-road folklore mood. No racing cars, engine flames, guns, wolves, combat queue, magic runes or gem currency. One image only, no concept board, no marketing heading.

## Review

Both outputs show a horse-drawn salt cart, two mounted human pursuers, forest obstacles, lower steering/action controls and a proximity warning. The portrait demonstrates closing distance; landscape demonstrates a drifting bag throw and broken pursuit. Exact cargo/hold values are examples. The landscape also retains small utility icons from the style reference; they do not define additional racing mechanics. These are static illustrations, not implemented physics, multiplayer or validated input layouts.
